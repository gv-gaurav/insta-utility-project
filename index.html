Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding Insta Utility advisory scope and engagement terms.

Q1. What does Insta Utility do?
We provide advisory support across three areas: renewable energy planning, CCTS/carbon market readiness, and GHG accounting & decarbonisation roadmaps.

No. We operate as an independent advisory firm and do not certify, verify, or accredit any project, credit, or claim.

No. We do not act as a broker or trading intermediary for carbon credits, Green Credits, or RECs.
