⚡ Group Captive Structure Screening

Who this is for

Multi-entity groups, larger continuous loads (often ~1 MW+), and energy decision-makers evaluating whether a captive / group-captive equity structure deserves a separate compliance and structure review.

What Group Captive means here

Group Captive structures involve ownership, equity participation, and compliance requirements under applicable captive guidelines. Thresholds and exemptions (including any CSS treatment) must be verified case by case. This tool and page do not confirm eligibility.

Diagnostic Screening & Next Steps

Understanding diagnostic routing and structure next steps.

How screening relates to the free diagnostic

When demand and profile signals support it, the diagnostic may recommend Group Captive Screening as a primary or parallel path. That is a screening recommendation only not a legal or tax determination.

Next steps after a GC-oriented result

Complete evidence collection (bills, demand, entity structure notes), then consider the Paid Renewable Power Diagnostic for a deeper memo. Structure, equity, and developer engagement remain separate workstreams.

Evidence requirements

Same free-screen evidence pack; plus optional notes on multi-entity / group structure if available.

Exclusions

Does not confirm 26% or any equity threshold. Does not certify CSS/AS exemption. Not legal, tax, or investment advice. No binding PPA or developer award from this page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding Group Captive screening.

Does a Group Captive screen mean we are CSS-exempt?
No. Captive compliance and any surcharge treatment require separate verification.
Can small single-entity sites use this page?
They can read it, but the free diagnostic often routes smaller or incomplete profiles to Open Access screening or Audit-first rather than Group Captive.
Is there a fixed equity or tariff figure on this page?
No fixed commercial tariff or guaranteed equity outcome is published here.
