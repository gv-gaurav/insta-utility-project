Who it is for

C&I Facilities / Plant / CFO / Procurement decision-makers (roughly 100 kW-5 MW) who have run the free screen and need a Stage 2 memo before developer or supply-structure decisions.

What you receive

  • Written diagnostic memo: baseline summary from your evidence
  • Comparative pathway screening (DISCOM / Open Access / Group Captive / rooftop qualitative)
  • Data-gap list and recommended next engineering steps
  • Risk / exclusion appendix

What we need from you

  • 12-month DISCOM bills (or best available)
  • Contracted demand; state/DISCOM; supply mode
  • Named decision contact and consent
  • Optional: TOD notes, roof/land constraints, group-entity structure

Timeline & commercial

Target memo window typically 5-10 working days after complete evidence (complexity-dependent). Fee and exact timeline confirmed only after scope lock no fixed price is published on this page. Free screen remains free and creates no purchase obligation.
