Stage 2 Scope & Deliverables

Comprehensive evidence-grounded assessment for C&I decision-makers.

Who It Is For

C&I Facilities Heads, Plant Managers, CFOs, and Procurement decision-makers (roughly 100 kW – 5 MW contracted demand) who have run the free screen and need a formal Stage 2 diagnostic memo before developer or supply-structure decisions.

What You Receive (Deliverables)

  • Written Diagnostic Memo: In-depth baseline summary derived from your actual bill evidence.
  • Comparative Pathway Screening: Qualitative comparison across DISCOM grid, Open Access, Group Captive, and Rooftop solar options.
  • Data-Gap List: Identification of missing engineering/metering data and recommended next steps.
  • Risk & Exclusion Appendix: Comprehensive regulatory risk disclosure and compliance guidance.

What We Need From You

  • 12-Month DISCOM Bills: PDF, ZIP, PNG, CSV, or XLSX uploads (or best available bills).
  • Demand Details: Contracted demand (kW), state/DISCOM name, and current supply mode.
  • Decision Contact: Named contact, role, corporate email, phone, and consent.
  • Optional Extras: Time-of-Day (TOD) bill breakdown, rooftop/land availability notes, multi-entity group structure.

Timeline & Commercial Terms

Target memo turnaround window is typically 5–10 working days after receipt of complete evidence pack (complexity-dependent).

Fee and exact turnaround timeline are confirmed only after initial scope lock. No fixed public price is published on this website.

* Running the free screen is completely free and creates zero purchase obligation.

Scope Exclusions & Legal Disclaimers

The Paid Diagnostic is not a savings guarantee. It does not provide legal certification of Open Access or Group Captive eligibility, is not a developer RFP or PPA contract, and does not include public fixed pricing or automatic CRM owner assignment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding the Paid Renewable Power Diagnostic.

Is the free screen required before requesting a Paid Diagnostic?
Strongly recommended so route guidance and Submission_Ref context exist. Direct paid enquiries can still be scoped, but evidence completeness still gates the start of formal work.
Do you guarantee savings in the Stage 2 report?
No. Under our No Quote policy, this engagement does not guarantee a savings percentage or fixed ₹/kWh rate. All analysis provides qualitative risk and tariff structure evaluation.
What items are strictly excluded from scope?
Binding PPAs, legal/tax opinions, structural civil engineering, DISCOM application filings, and automatic CRM owner assignment are explicitly out of scope.
