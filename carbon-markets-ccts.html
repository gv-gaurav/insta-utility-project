Target Reader / Problem

Who Needs CCTS Advisory Support?

Obligated entities, PAT transition units, and industrial entities needing clarity on CCTS compliance mechanisms.

🏭 The CCTS Compliance Challenge

Entities that may fall under India's Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS) — including obligated entities transitioning from the PAT framework, and other businesses seeking to understand carbon market mechanisms — who need clarity on obligations, timelines, and preparation steps, but lack in-house carbon market expertise.

Scope of Work

What This Service Covers

  • Explaining CCTS applicability to the client's sector/entity.
  • Helping clients understand target-setting mechanisms and compliance-cycle timelines, as publicly notified.
  • Supporting internal readiness assessment (data, systems, processes) for CCTS compliance.
  • Supporting documentation and record-keeping practices aligned with public regulatory requirements.
  • General awareness / orientation sessions for client teams.
