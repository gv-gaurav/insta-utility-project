Who this is for

Facilities heads, plant managers, CFOs, and procurement leads at Indian C&I sites roughly 100 kW–5 MW contracted demand currently on DISCOM HT supply who want an initial Open Access pathway screen.

What Open Access means here

Third-party Open Access is a route where power may be procured from a generator or trader wheeled over the grid under state procedures. Feasibility depends on demand, state rules, charges (including CSS/AS where applicable), credit, and counterparty arrangements. This page does not approve a switch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding Open Access eligibility screening.

Is this a guaranteed tariff or savings offer?
Insta Utility operates under No Quote , no savings percentage or fixed Rs/kWh is guaranteed or published as a commercial offer on this page.
What demand level is typically relevant?
The diagnostic is designed around C&I facilities in roughly the 100 kW–5 MW contracted demand range. Sub-100 kW profiles are usually directed to audit-first / data collection rather than a strong OA push.
Does the free screen confirm Open Access approval?
No. It is an informational eligibility screen only. State procedures, DISCOM processes, and counterparty checks remain separate.
