Target Reader / Problem

Who Needs Renewable Energy Advisory?

Commercial and industrial consumers seeking to reduce grid power dependence and energy costs without internal procurement teams.

🏭 The Challenge for C&I Energy Users

Commercial and industrial consumers who want to reduce dependence on conventional grid power or reduce energy costs through renewable energy routes (e.g., rooftop solar, open access, group captive), but are unsure which option fits their site, load profile, and regulatory context, and lack an internal team to evaluate options.

Scope of Work

What This Service Covers

  • Assessment of renewable energy options relevant to the client's load and site (rooftop, ground-mounted, open access / group captive, etc., as applicable and available).
  • Review of the applicable state/central regulatory framework for the client's situation.
  • Feasibility-level evaluation support.
  • Support in structuring requirements before approaching developers, vendors, or open-access processes.
  • Advisory-level review support for proposals or PPAs the client has already received (review only, not legal drafting).
Placeholder note: Precise scope to be tailored per engagement, site, and state; not all options may be applicable everywhere.

📋 Inputs Needed From Client

  • Site address(es) and available area / rooftop details (if rooftop)
  • Historical energy consumption data (e.g., 12 months of bills or load data)
  • Contract demand / sanctioned load
  • Ownership status of site / rooftop
  • Budget range or investment appetite
  • Any existing vendor/developer proposals, if available

📦 Deliverables Offered

  • Written feasibility / options advisory note
  • Summary of applicable regulatory considerations as understood at the time of engagement
  • Checklist/questions the client can use while evaluating vendors or open-access opportunities
Process

6-Step Assessment Workflow

6

Data Collection & Review

7

8

10

Formal delivery of final advisory deliverable and evaluation checklist.

⚠️ Exclusions & Advisory Limitations

  • Does not act as an EPC contractor, project developer, or financier.
  • Does not guarantee any specific generation output, cost savings, payback period, or return on investment.
  • Does not provide legal or tax advice.
  • Does not draft or negotiate contracts on the client's behalf.
  • Findings are based on client-provided information and publicly available regulations at the time of engagement; not a substitute for statutory approvals.
