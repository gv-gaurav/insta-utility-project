Target Reader / Problem

Who Needs GHG & MRV Advisory?

Companies measuring greenhouse gas emissions for internal management, stakeholder reporting, or compliance readiness.

🏭 The Emissions Accounting Challenge

Companies that need to measure and report their greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions — for internal management, stakeholder or investor requests, upcoming compliance requirements, or voluntary disclosure — but lack an internal GHG inventory, MRV (Measurement, Reporting, Verification) systems, or a structured decarbonisation plan.

Scope of Work

What This Service Covers

  • Support in building a GHG emissions inventory (Scope 1 and Scope 2, and Scope 3 where feasible) based on client-provided activity data and recognised public methodologies (e.g., GHG Protocol).
  • Advisory on setting up internal MRV processes and data systems.
  • Support in developing a decarbonisation roadmap outlining potential intervention areas (energy efficiency, fuel switching, renewable adoption, etc.) for client consideration.
Placeholder note: Specific standards/frameworks applied are confirmed per engagement.

📋 Inputs Needed From Client

  • Activity data (fuel consumption, electricity bills, process data, etc.) for the relevant boundary/period
  • Organisational boundary information (facilities, ownership structure)
  • Existing sustainability/ESG reporting, if any
  • Internal team availability for data validation

📦 Deliverables Offered

  • GHG inventory summary (advisory-level, based on data provided by the client)
  • MRV framework / process document
  • Decarbonisation roadmap outlining potential focus areas for the client's consideration
