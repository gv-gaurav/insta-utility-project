## 1. Product Name
**Paid Renewable Power Diagnostic v1** — Detailed engineering & tariff assessment (Stage 2)

## 2. Target Buyer Persona
Indian C&I decision-makers (Facilities / Plant / CFO / Procurement) at sites roughly 100 kW–5 MW contracted demand who completed the free eligibility screen and want a deeper, evidence-based review before engaging developers or changing supply structure.

## 3. Required Evidence Checklist
- 12 months DISCOM bills (or certified summary) preferred; minimum recent monthly bills if full year unavailable
- Contracted / sanctioned demand (kW) and supply voltage class if known
- State / DISCOM and site address or industrial area
- Current supply mode (DISCOM only / existing OA / captive)
- Named decision contact and consent to process commercial data
- *Optional:* load profile, TOD pattern notes, roof/land constraints, group-entity structure

## 4. Deliverables
- **Written diagnostic memo:** demand/tariff baseline summary from provided evidence
- **Pathway screen:** DISCOM baseline vs third-party Open Access vs Group Captive vs rooftop/BTM — qualitative feasibility only
- **Data-gap list** and recommended next engineering steps
- **Risk / exclusion appendix** (CSS, AS, wheeling, banking, eligibility not certified)
- **Optional short clarification call** (scope fixed at engagement)

## 5. Explicit Exclusions
- Not a binding PPA, tariff guarantee, or legal/tax opinion
- No guaranteed savings percentage or fixed ₹/kWh quote in v1
- Does not confirm Group Captive 26% equity eligibility or CSS exemption
- Does not replace DISCOM applications, developer RFP, or structural engineering
- CRM stores only approved fields; Owner not auto-assigned

## 6. Indicative Timeline
- Kick-off after evidence pack received and engagement confirmed
- Target memo delivery: 5–10 working days from complete evidence (complexity-dependent)
- Exact timeline confirmed in the proposal reply — not published as a hard SLA on the website

## 7. Commercial Terms
- **Fee:** quoted only after scope lock — **no fixed price published on staging or in this pack**
- **Payment / PO terms:** as agreed in proposal confirmation
- Free eligibility screen remains free and does not obligate purchase of Stage 2
